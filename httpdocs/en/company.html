Overview

NHAN VIET ENGINEERING TM DV CO LTD

NHAN VIET ENGINEERING TM DV CO LTD was established in 2020 and is headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, Viet Nam.

Our operation motto is to deliver optimal and safe deployment solutions with flexible strategies, bringing the core values that customers need the most.

  • Target customers include manufacturing enterprises, transportation services, schools, banks, consumer commercial stores and state-sector units.
  • We constantly update new policies and technologies from brand labels to promote suitable solutions to customers as quickly as possible.
  • We provide products, software, project deployment and after-sales services within one practical solution system.

Nhan Viet Company Development

Key milestones from establishment to market expansion, project implementation and internal operation upgrades.

2020

Established Nhan Viet Company

Sales started in VN-Singapore II-A Industrial Zone and Tan Tao Industrial Zone. Principle contracts were signed to supply goods every year to customers.

2021

Network Equipment Project

Completed a network equipment project with a logistics group for five sites: Ho Chi Minh City, Quy Nhon, Ha Noi and Da Nang. Expanded sales to Thanh Thanh Cong and Bau Bang Industrial Zones.

2022

Market and Brand Cooperation

Completed BMS project at Nam Tan Uyen and continued expanding sales to My Xuan B1-CONAC, Duc Hoa II and My Xuan Industrial Zones while building cooperation with brands in Viet Nam.

2023

Branch and Process Improvement

Expanded a new branch in Ho Chi Minh City, improved sales and after-sales processes, completed Dell EMC warranty work at Bau Bang, Go Truong Chinh electrical and water works, and server/switch system at My Xuan Industrial Zone.

2024 - 2025

Projects and Training

Completed CCTV, audio and switchboard systems at Bac Lieu Court, expanded sales to Duc Hoa III and Phu An Thanh Industrial Zones, recruited and standardized training, and deployed MEP electrical work for Hoan My International Hospital.

2026

Scale and Operation Upgrade

Expanding company scale, deploying MEP electrical projects, renewing maintenance contracts at industrial parks, upgrading internal operations and customer communication, and developing better sales policies.
